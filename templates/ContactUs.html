What is a computer virus?

A computer virus is a malicious software program designed to infect a computer system, replicate itself, and spread to other devices. It typically attaches itself to legitimate programs or files and can be triggered when a user unknowingly runs the infected software. Once activated, a virus can cause various forms of harm, such as corrupting data, slowing down system performance, stealing personal information, or even rendering a system unusable. Viruses spread through email attachments, downloads, and compromised websites, making it crucial for users to maintain strong cybersecurity practices and use updated antivirus software to protect their devices.

How do I know if my system is infected?

Look for signs such as unexpected slowdowns, frequent crashes, or system freezes. Unusual pop-up messages, new or unfamiliar programs starting up automatically, or changes to your homepage or browser settings can also be indicators. Running a full system scan with a reputable antivirus program is the most reliable way to detect infections.
