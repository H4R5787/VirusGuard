About VirusGuard

Enterprise-grade automated cybersecurity intelligence and multi-engine threat detection.

Our Origin & Purpose

Designed and engineered by Harsh Vishwakarma, VirusGuard was built to solve a persistent cybersecurity challenge: enterprise malware detection often produces cryptic hexadecimal hashes and siloed scanner signatures that confuse developers and systems engineers. By marrying multi-engine threat telemetry (VirusTotal API v3) with automated entity extraction, live threat feed correlation (CISA, BleepingComputer, The Hacker News), and LLM-powered intelligence briefings, VirusGuard delivers actionable containment guidance at enterprise speed.

Zero-Persistence Quarantine

Sub-Second Triage

Automated Threat Intel

Daily midnight ingestion synchronizes real-world CVEs and active Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).
